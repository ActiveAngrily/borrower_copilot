Your assessment

Start with the borrowing need

Step 1 1 of 7

Only relevant questions appear. A blank or “I don’t know” answer is never treated as zero.

Borrowing need

01 What is the loan for?

This selects the supported illustrative product path.

02 How much do you want to borrow?

Use contractual principal, not cash left after fees.

Household income

03 What net income is available now?

Use income after work costs and taxes, before household spending and debt. Do not include hoped-for income from this loan.

04 Does someone contribute to this same budget?

Enter 0 only when there is no contribution. Do not enter another person’s full salary unless it is actually available here.

Household commitments

05 What does the household spend?

Include housing, food, utilities, transport and people supported. Exclude debt and costs already removed from income.

06 What debt payments are required?

Use required payments, not outstanding balances. A resolved bounce is different from a currently unpaid one.

07 What is your age?

No date of birth is needed. Unknown age keeps product eligibility unresolved.

Credit context

08 What do you know about your credit record?

No score is invented and no score-based rate discount is assumed.

Security

09 Can this property support the selected path?

Property can limit a lender estimate. It never becomes household repayment income.

Vehicle

09 What is the on-road vehicle price?

The illustrative path finances at most 85%; fees and other constraints can require more own funds.

Funding

10 What must the borrowing pay for?

Loan fees and the purpose cannot spend the same rupee twice.

Would a smaller version still work?

A “Borrow less” result needs a genuinely useful smaller plan, not just a lower EMI.

Income history

What was the lowest recent net-income month?

Use the latest available period, up to 12 completed months, after work or business costs. It is tested separately—not reduced by another 20%.

Productive use

What additional net income could the purchase create?

Enter income after added operating costs, downtime and displaced earnings. It will never raise current safe capacity.

Resilience and offer

What emergency money remains after this plan?

Optional. Reserves show temporary coverage; they never increase recurring capacity.

Is a known one-time expense due within 12 months?
Do you have a lender offer to compare?

Only one-disbursement monthly reducing or explicitly flat-rate offers are supported.

Actual lender offer

What are the contractual repayment terms?
Are all mandatory charges included?

Use 0 only where the offer confirms no charge.
