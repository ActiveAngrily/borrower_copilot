# Stage 15 Persona Run-throughs

These runs use only facts supplied by the assignment. They do not invent expenses, debt payments, contributions, evidence, fees, offer terms, reserves, security consent, or projected earnings to force a verdict. Each run therefore completes the interaction with honest `Not estimable` or conditional outputs where required.

## Priya — salaried personal loan for a wedding

### Supplied facts entered

- Age: 29.
- Current monthly net income: ₹1,10,000.
- Required car EMI: ₹14,000 per month, with two years left.
- Credit score is reported known as 780; no numerical discount is inferred from it.
- Requested amount: ₹8,00,000 for a wedding, routed to the personal-loan path.
- Rent is ₹28,000, but rent is not entered as total household spending.

### Questions asked by the adaptive flow

1. Purpose and requested amount.
2. Current net-income range, income type, variability, supporting records, and household contribution.
3. Total household spending, required debt payments, applicant-debt scope, repayment-problem status, and age.
4. Credit-score availability.
5. Total wedding cost, own funds, and whether a smaller wedding plan would still work.
6. Optional reserves, upcoming commitments, and actual-offer availability.

The property, electric-vehicle, and productive-income panels do not appear. The income-history panel appears only if Priya confirms material income variation.

### Four outputs

| Output | Result | Why / next question |
|---|---|---|
| Safe borrower capacity | **Not estimable** | Total household spending and confirmed household contribution are unknown. Ask for all recurring spending and whether any other money is actually available to this budget. |
| Likely lender sanction | **Not estimable** | Income evidence and the complete applicant/co-borrower debt total are unresolved. Ask what records support the ₹1,10,000 figure and whether ₹14,000 covers every applicant obligation. |
| Recommended borrowing amount | **Not estimable; assessment incomplete** | Safe capacity and full wedding funding cannot be checked. Ask for total wedding cost, own funds, repayment status, and smaller-plan viability. |
| Price / offer comparison | **Conditional benchmark APR: 9.99%–25.01%** | This is the 36-month personal-loan benchmark with the documented illustrative fee package, not Priya's offer. A complete KFS is required for actual APR. |

Reference payment for the requested amount at the upper benchmark rate is approximately **₹31,386/month for 36 months**. This is not a safe-payment finding.

## Run-through conclusion

The named personas are deliberately incomplete because the assignment does not provide every decision-driving fact. The app remains useful by showing the exact dependency that blocks each output, an independent conditional price reference, and the next questions to take forward. Complete synthetic cases in `rules.test.mjs` separately prove that `Borrow`, `Borrow less`, and `Do not borrow under assessed conditions` are all reachable without rewriting persona facts.
